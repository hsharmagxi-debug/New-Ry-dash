# RYDASH 2026 — World & Visual System

## World selection
RYDASH now presents all eight playable environments as visual cards with:
- world image
- location
- difficulty
- weather identity
- track length
- large selected-world preview

## World phases
Every world supports four presentation phases:
- DAY
- SUNSET
- NIGHT
- STORM

The selected phase is persisted locally and applied to the real Three.js race scene through:
- sky/background color
- fog color
- scene light intensity
- material environment readability
- race HUD world/phase identity

## Race readability
A neutral sky fill and moving player-car key light improve visibility of:
- body paint
- rear lights
- wheels
- body silhouette
- track surface

The race renderer also uses a higher exposure target on high quality.
