    HOW TO PLAY

    • W / ↑ — Accelerate
    • S / ↓ — Brake / Reverse
    • A D / ← → — Steer
    • Space — Handbrake (drift)
    • Shift — Nitro boost
    • C — Change camera
    • Esc — Pause

    On mobile, use the on-screen touch controls. A connected gamepad is auto-detected — left stick steers, triggers gas/brake, A/Cross for nitro, B/Circle or bumpers for handbrake.

    Cars now bump each other on contact — ram opponents off their line to take the lead.

    Your fastest single-player run on each world+car is saved locally as a translucent ghost to race against next time — beat it to save a new best.

    The Skyline world has real launch ramps — hit one fast enough and you'll get real air.
